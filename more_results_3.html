Ours on HDTF

23 HDTF clips at their natural length (11–43 s). The input is the first frame of each clip and the audio is the clip's own speech; nothing else from the video is used. Rows: input portrait, frontal rendering, and the same avatar sequence from a moving camera: a full turn around the head, or a sweep across the viewing range the training cameras cover. Videos autoplay muted; Audio unmutes one subject at a time.
